Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

Android package: com.speakeratlas.speaker_atlas

Privacy at a glance

SpeakerAtlas is an offline-first setup and troubleshooting guide. The reviewed app does not require accounts and does not contain advertising or analytics SDKs. Bookmarks and checklist progress are stored locally on your device.

1. About SpeakerAtlas

SpeakerAtlas is an independently developed educational guide. It provides instructions and troubleshooting references for smart speakers and related devices. It is not an official manufacturer application and cannot pair with or control speakers on your behalf.

2. Information stored locally

The application saves selected guide bookmarks and completed setup steps using Android application preferences (SharedPreferences). This information supports saved guides and progress tracking on your device.

Model searches and brand filters are used locally to display relevant guide information. The reviewed app does not send these searches to a SpeakerAtlas-operated server.

3. Personal information and collection

The reviewed version does not require your name, email address, phone number, account credentials or registration. It does not request access to your microphone, camera, contacts or precise location. No advertising or behavioral analytics SDKs are included in the reviewed app.

SpeakerAtlas does not operate a backend that collects your bookmarks, setup progress or search history.

4. Data sharing

SpeakerAtlas does not sell or share locally stored bookmarks or checklist information with advertising partners. Your saved guide records are not transmitted to a SpeakerAtlas developer-operated server.

5. External manufacturer websites

Some guides include optional links to manufacturer support websites. If you choose to open one, it opens through your browser. That website may process your IP address, device or browser information under its own privacy policy. SpeakerAtlas does not control third-party websites.

6. Device backups

Depending on Android and device backup settings, application preferences may be included in operating-system backups. These backups are managed by Android or your device provider, not by a SpeakerAtlas-operated cloud service.

7. Retention and deletion

Bookmarks and completed checklist steps remain locally until removed. To delete them, open About SpeakerAtlas inside the app and select Clear bookmarks and progress. You may also clear the app's storage through Android Settings or uninstall the application.

Operating-system backups, if enabled, are controlled through your device or backup-provider settings.

8. Security

SpeakerAtlas does not ask for smart speaker account passwords. Core guides work offline, and external support links use HTTPS. Local data security depends on device and operating-system protections. No system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's privacy

SpeakerAtlas is a general-purpose reference guide and is not designed as a child-directed service. The reviewed app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Website hosting

This policy website is intended to be hosted on Netlify. The static website does not intentionally include advertising cookies or third-party analytics. Netlify and its infrastructure may process website request information for delivery and security. Review Netlify's Privacy Policy for details.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features, permissions, services or data practices change. The effective date will be updated when changes are published.
